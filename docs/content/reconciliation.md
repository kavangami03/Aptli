## What is OTA reconciliation?
OTA reconciliation is checking what an online travel agency billed or paid against what your PMS shows for the same stay. For each reservation it compares the status, dates, nights, revenue, commission and payment. When the two records disagree, the hotel has either been overcharged or has money it has not collected yet.

For most hotels this covers three money flows. There are OTA commission invoices from Expedia, Booking.com and Hotels.com, the virtual cards guests prepaid through, and direct-bill accounts such as CLC crew lodging. Aptli reconciles all three in one daily check, so you don't need a separate tool or vendor for each channel.
- For owners: one view of what each channel owes you.
- For GMs and controllers: nothing new for the front desk. Aptli reads what is already in your systems.

## How does Aptli reconcile OTA bookings every day?
Each day Aptli reads new and changed reservations from the OTA extranets, the virtual card details, the CLC portal and your PMS. It pairs each OTA or CLC record with its PMS reservation and compares the stay and the money line by line. Every difference becomes a finding with both records attached.

- Read the records: OTA extranets, virtual cards, the CLC portal and your PMS
- Pair each reservation: by confirmation number first
- Compare the stay and the money: status, nights, dates, amounts and payments
- Flag every difference: as a finding with the OTA and PMS records side by side

## What is an exact match vs a fuzzy match?
An exact match is when the OTA or CLC confirmation number points to one reservation in your PMS, so both records clearly describe the same stay. A fuzzy match is when that number is missing or different, and Aptli pairs the records using details such as guest name, stay dates and amount instead.

Exact matches can move to the next step under the rules you set. Fuzzy matches never act on their own. A person on your team confirms the pair first, because a wrong pair would mean disputing the wrong stay.

## Should hotels reconcile OTAs daily or monthly?
Daily. A monthly check finds problems weeks after the stay, when some OTA dispute and reporting windows may already be closed and the details are harder to prove. A daily check finds the same problems while the folio is fresh, so disputes go out in time and money arrives sooner. Monthly totals still matter for closing the books.

| | Monthly reconciliation | Daily reconciliation with Aptli |
|---|---|---|
| When problems are found | Weeks after check-out | Soon after the records change |
| Dispute and reporting windows | Some may have closed | Still open in most cases (check each OTA's current terms) |
| Evidence | Gathered by hand later | OTA and PMS records attached to each finding |
| Work for your team | A spreadsheet project at month-end | A short list of findings to review |
| Month-end close | Surprises to chase | Most issues already handled |

## FAQ
### How often should a hotel reconcile OTA bookings?
Daily is best. Commission errors, uncharged virtual cards and unposted CLC payments are easiest to fix while the stay is recent and OTA dispute windows are still open. Many hotels reconcile monthly because the work is manual. Aptli runs the check every day, so your team only reviews the findings that need them.

### Which channels does Aptli reconcile?
Aptli reconciles OTA commissions from Expedia, Booking.com and Hotels.com, OTA virtual cards, and CLC direct-bill crew stays and payments. Each one is matched against your PMS reservation by reservation, so commission, card and crew-lodging leaks show up in one daily view instead of three separate reports.

### Does reconciliation change anything in my PMS?
No. Reconciliation only reads your PMS, the OTA extranets and the CLC portal to compare records. Nothing is posted or changed during this step. Actions such as filing a dispute or posting a payment happen later, under rules you set for each property, and the ones that need approval wait for you.

### What is a fuzzy match, and is it safe?
A fuzzy match pairs an OTA or CLC record with a PMS reservation using details like guest name, stay dates and amount, because the confirmation number is missing or different. It is safe because Aptli never acts on a fuzzy match by itself. A person on your team confirms the pair before anything moves forward.

### Do I need to replace my PMS or channel manager?
No. Aptli works alongside the PMS you already run, including choiceADVANTAGE, HotelKey, SynXis, OPERA Cloud and AutoClerk. Some connectors are live and others are rolling out, so tell us yours when you book the free leakage review and we will confirm the setup for your property.

### How is this different from an OTA statement or a monthly report?
An OTA statement shows what the OTA billed, and a monthly report lists mismatches after the fact. Aptli compares every reservation against your PMS each day and attaches both records to each finding as proof. Then it hands the finding on to be fixed, so your team is not left chasing a spreadsheet.

### Can Aptli reconcile more than one property?
Yes. Each property is connected with its own consent and its own rules, and the findings roll up so a management company can see every property in one place. You can start with one property in the free leakage review and add the others once you have seen the findings.
