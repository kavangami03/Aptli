# Image licence record (Aptli website)

**Purpose:** a single record that every photo on the Aptli website comes from **Pexels**, a free stock photo library, and is used under the **Pexels License**. Keep this file with the project. If anyone ever raises a copyright question about an image, this file shows where the image came from, when, and under which licence.

- **Source of every photo:** [pexels.com](https://www.pexels.com). No images come from Pinterest, Google Images, Unsplash, Shutterstock or any other site.
- **Licence:** Pexels License, [pexels.com/license](https://www.pexels.com/license/)
- **Downloaded:** November 2026, for the Aptli website build. This file was compiled on 13 November 2026.
- **Downloaded from:** the official Pexels image server (`images.pexels.com/photos/<id>/…`). Each file was then resized and compressed for the web. Editing is allowed under the licence.
- **Logos and brand marks:** the Aptli logo files in `public/brand/` belong to the client and are not stock images. No third-party logos are used as images.

## 1. The Pexels License, in Pexels' own words

Checked on 13 November 2026 at [pexels.com/license](https://www.pexels.com/license/):

**Allowed**
- "All photos and videos on Pexels are free to use."
- "Attribution is not required." Credit is optional and "always appreciated".
- "You can modify the photos and videos from Pexels."
- Commercial use is allowed, for example ads, banners, marketing campaigns and websites.

**Not allowed**
- "Identifiable people may not appear in a bad light or in a way that is offensive."
- "Don't sell unaltered copies of a photo or video, e.g. as a poster, print or on a physical product."
- "Don't imply endorsement of your product by people or brands on the imagery."
- "Don't use the photos or videos as part of your trade-mark, design-mark, trade-name, business name or service mark."
- "Don't redistribute or sell the photos and videos on other stock photo or wallpaper platforms."

### How the Aptli site keeps to these rules
- Photos are used only as website illustrations. They are never sold, printed for sale, or uploaded to other stock sites.
- **No stock photo is paired with a customer quote, a name, a job title or a testimonial.** That would imply the person in the photo endorses Aptli. Customer quotes must use approved photos of the real customer (see WEBSITE-STANDARDS.md §6).
- No stock photo is used in the Aptli logo, brand mark or trade name.
- People are shown in a neutral or positive, professional setting.
- Photos showing a visible third-party hotel name, sign or logo are avoided. **Known exception to fix:** `home/cta-hotel-dusk.jpg` (closing call to action on every page) shows part of a hotel's rooftop sign. Replace it with a photo with no readable name before launch.

## 3. Extra proof worth keeping

Pexels can change its licence, and a photographer can remove a photo, in the future. The licence that applied on the day of download still covers that copy. A dated snapshot makes this easy to show, so the strongest record is:

1. **Save the licence page.** Open https://www.pexels.com/license/ and use File → Print → Save as PDF. Name it `pexels-license-2026-11-13.pdf` and store it with this file.
2. **Save each photo page.** Open each Pexels link above and save it as a PDF the same way. The page shows the photographer and the free download.
3. **Optional public timestamp.** Paste each link into the Internet Archive's "Save Page Now" (web.archive.org/save). It stores a dated public copy.
4. **Keep a backup of the image files.** Copy `src/assets/images/` to your company drive with this file and the PDFs, so the full set is stored together outside the code.

## 4. Rules for adding photos later

- Download only from **pexels.com** (or another library whose licence clearly allows free commercial use), never from Google Images, Pinterest or social media.
- Add a row to the table above **the same day**: file, where it is used, the Pexels page link.
- Never pair a stock photo with a quote, testimonial, name or job title.
- Avoid photos that show another company's logo, sign or product name.
- Use each photo once across the site.
